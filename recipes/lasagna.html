Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta sheets, rich meat or vegetable sauce, creamy béchamel or cheese, and a blend of flavorful seasonings. Baked until golden and bubbling, it is known for its hearty texture, comforting taste, and satisfying combination of ingredients. Popular around the world, lasagna is often enjoyed as a family meal and can be customized with a variety of fillings, from traditional beef and tomato sauce to vegetarian alternatives.

Ingredients

  • Minced meat
  • Spaghetti sauce
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
